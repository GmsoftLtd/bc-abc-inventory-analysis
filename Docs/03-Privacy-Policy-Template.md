## Introduction

GMSOFT ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our ABC Analysis extension ("the Extension") for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central ("Business Central").

This Extension is available through Microsoft AppSource and operates within your Business Central environment.

**By installing and using the Extension, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.**

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Business Data
The Extension processes the following data from your Business Central environment to provide inventory analysis functionality:

**Item Information**:
- Item numbers and descriptions
- Unit costs and prices
- Inventory quantities
- Sales history
- Purchase history
- Location data

**Analysis Data**:
- ABC classifications generated
- XYZ demand variability calculations
- Historical analysis results
- User-defined parameters and thresholds

**Important**: This data remains in your Business Central environment and is NOT transmitted to GMSOFT servers.

### 2. Telemetry Data
We collect anonymous usage telemetry through Azure Application Insights to improve the Extension:

**Usage Information**:
- Feature usage statistics (e.g., which reports are run)
- Error logs and exceptions
- Performance metrics
- Session duration and frequency
- Business Central version and environment type

**Technical Information**:
- Extension version
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Device type
- Locale and language settings

**This telemetry data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify individual users or companies.**

### 3. Support Data
When you contact our support team, we may collect:
- Your name and email address
- Company name
- Issue description
- Screenshots or files you provide
- Correspondence history

### 4. Account Information
If you create an account on our support portal or website:
- Name and email address
- Company information
- License and subscription details

- **To provide the Extension functionality**: Perform ABC/XYZ analysis, generate reports, and display results

- **Provide customer support**: Respond to inquiries and troubleshoot issues

### Account Information
- **Manage licenses**: Activate and manage your subscription
- **Send communications**: Product updates, renewal reminders, marketing (with consent)
- **Comply with legal obligations**: Tax reporting, license compliance

## Data Storage and Security

### Business Data
- **Stored in your environment**: All business data remains in your Business Central database
- **Microsoft's responsibility**: Data security is governed by your Business Central subscription terms
- **No GMSOFT access**: We do not have access to your business data

### Telemetry Data
- **Azure Application Insights**: Stored in Microsoft Azure (Western Europe region)
- **Retention period**: 90 days
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- **Access controls**: Limited to authorized GMSOFT personnel only

### Support Data
- **Secure storage**: Stored in our support system with encryption
- **Retention period**: Kept for 3 years or as required by law
- **Access controls**: Limited to support staff only
