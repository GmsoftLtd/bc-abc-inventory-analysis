## 4. Licensing Model

### Per-Tenant Licensing
**What is a Tenant?**
- A tenant = one Business Central environment
- Each production or sandbox environment requires a separate license

**Examples**:

**Scenario 1: Single Production Environment**
- Company has 1 production BC tenant
- **License needed**: 1 subscription

**Scenario 2: Production + Sandbox**
- Company has 1 production + 1 sandbox tenant
- **License needed**: 2 subscriptions (or use trial on sandbox)

**Scenario 3: Multi-Company within One Tenant**
- Company has 5 companies in 1 BC tenant
- **License needed**: 1 subscription (Professional or Enterprise)

**Scenario 4: Multiple Legal Entities**
- Holding company with 3 subsidiaries, each with own BC tenant
- **License needed**: 3 subscriptions

### User-Based Licensing?
**No.** There is NO per-user limit. Any number of users within the tenant can use the extension.

### Company Limits
- **Basic Plan**: 1 company
- **Professional Plan**: Unlimited companies
- **Enterprise Plan**: Unlimited companies

### Item Limits
- **Basic Plan**: Up to 5,000 items
- **Professional Plan**: Unlimited items
- **Enterprise Plan**: Unlimited items

## 6. Billing and Payments

### Payment Methods

**Credit/Debit Card**:
- Visa, MasterCard, American Express
- Automatic monthly/annual charges
- Processed by Microsoft

**Invoice Billing**:
- Available for annual subscriptions
- Requires credit check by Microsoft
- NET 30 payment terms
- Minimum: Enterprise Plan annual subscription

### Billing Cycle

**Monthly Subscriptions**:
- Billed on the same day each month
- Example: Subscribe on Jan 15 → Next bill Feb 15
- Prorated for first partial month

**Annual Subscriptions**:
- Billed once per year on subscription date
- Example: Subscribe on Jan 15, 2025 → Next bill Jan 15, 2026
- 16% savings vs. monthly

### Automatic Renewal
- Subscriptions renew automatically
- Charged to payment method on file
- Email reminder sent 7 days before renewal
- Cancel anytime before renewal date to avoid charge

### Failed Payments
1. **Day 1**: Payment fails, automatic retry
2. **Day 3**: Second retry, email notification sent
3. **Day 7**: Final retry, warning email sent
4. **Day 10**: Subscription suspended (read-only mode)
5. **Day 30**: Subscription cancelled, extension disabled

**To Resolve**: Update payment method in Microsoft 365 Admin Center

### Invoices and Receipts
- Email receipt sent after each payment
- View invoices: Microsoft 365 Admin Center → Billing → Bills & Payments
- Download PDF for accounting

## 7. Upgrades and Downgrades

### Upgrading Plans

**From Basic to Professional**:
- Click "Upgrade" in Extension Management
- Or visit Partner Center/Microsoft 365 Admin
- Pay difference prorated for current month
- Immediate access to Professional features

**From Professional to Enterprise**:
- Same process as above
- Prorated billing adjustment
- Onboarding session scheduled

**Billing Impact**:
- **Monthly**: Prorated charge for remainder of month
- **Annual**: Prorated charge for remainder of year

**Example**:
- Basic annual: Paid $499.99 on Jan 1
- Upgrade to Professional on July 1 (6 months remaining)
- Credit for unused Basic: $249.99
- Charge for Professional (6 months): $500
- Net charge: $250.01

### Downgrading Plans

**From Enterprise to Professional** or **Professional to Basic**:
- Contact support@gmsoft.co.uk to request downgrade
- Takes effect at next renewal date
- No immediate downgrade (to avoid losing features mid-billing period)
- **OR** Cancel current plan and subscribe to lower plan (loses remaining prepaid period)

**Feature Impact**:
- Lose access to premium features
- Data from premium features preserved (read-only)
- Example: Historical comparisons still visible, but can't create new ones

## 8. Cancellation and Refunds

### How to Cancel

**Option 1: Microsoft 365 Admin Center**
1. Sign in: https://admin.microsoft.com
2. Go to: Billing → Your Products
3. Find: ABC Analysis
4. Click: Cancel subscription
5. Confirm cancellation

**Option 2: AppSource**
1. Sign in to AppSource
2. Go to: My Apps
3. Find: ABC Analysis
4. Click: Manage → Cancel

**Option 3: Contact Support**
- Email: support@gmsoft.co.uk
- Request cancellation
- We'll process within 1 business day

### Cancellation Timing
- **Takes effect**: End of current billing period
- **Access**: Continues until end of paid period
- **Example**: Cancel on Jan 15, paid through Feb 15 → Access until Feb 15

### Immediate Cancellation
- If you need immediate cancellation, uninstall the extension
- No refund for unused portion (except where required by law)

### Refund Policy

**Free Trial**:
- No charge, no refund needed

**Within 7 Days of Purchase** (Monthly subscriptions):
- Full refund available if not satisfied
- Email support@gmsoft.co.uk with reason
- Processed within 5-10 business days

**Within 30 Days of Purchase** (Annual subscriptions):
- Full refund available
- Email support@gmsoft.co.uk
- Processed within 5-10 business days

**After Refund Period**:
- No refunds for unused portions
- Subscription remains active until end of billing period
- Exception: Required by local consumer protection laws

**Required by Law**:
- EU: 14-day right of withdrawal (if service not yet started)
- Other jurisdictions: Comply with local regulations

### What Happens to Your Data?
- **During cancellation period**: Full access continues
- **After cancellation**: Extension disabled, data preserved in BC for 30 days
- **After 30 days**: Extension-specific data purged (your BC data unaffected)
