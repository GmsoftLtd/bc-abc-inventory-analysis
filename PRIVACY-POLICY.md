## 1. Introduction

GMSOFT LIMITED ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. By installing and using the ABC Inventory Analysis, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Data Processing Within Business Central

The ABC Inventory Analysis operates entirely within your Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central environment. The App processes the following types of data:

#### **Inventory Data** (processed, not collected)
- Item numbers, descriptions, and attributes
- Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) information
- Inventory transactions (value entries)
- Sales amounts, cost amounts, and quantities
- Item classifications and ABC analysis results
- Physical inventory counting period codes

#### **Configuration Data** (processed, not collected)
- ABC analysis setup and configuration settings
- Threshold percentages (A/B/C breakpoints)
- Analysis periods and date ranges
- Counting policy mappings

#### **Usage Data** (processed, not collected)
- ABC calculation history
- Classification changes over time
- User-initiated analysis runs
- System-generated timestamps

**Important**: All of the above data remains within your Business Central environment. We do **not** collect, extract, or transmit this data outside of your Business Central tenant.

### 2.2 Telemetry and Diagnostic Data

The App uses **Microsoft Application Insights** for telemetry purposes to monitor app health and improve functionality. The following technical data may be transmitted to Microsoft Azure:

- **App version number**
- **Error messages and exception logs**
- **Performance metrics** (calculation duration, memory usage)
- **Feature usage statistics** (which features are used, frequency)
- **Anonymous session identifiers**

**What We Do NOT Collect**:
- ❌ Personal identifiable information (PII)
- ❌ User names, email addresses, or login credentials
- ❌ Business data (item details, financial amounts, customer information)
- ❌ Company names or business identifiers
- ❌ Inventory values or quantities

### 2.3 Authentication Data

The App uses Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central's built-in authentication and authorization mechanisms. We do not collect, store, or process any authentication credentials. User authentication is handled entirely by Microsoft Azure Active Directory.

## 3. How We Use Information

### 3.1 Data Processing Purpose

We process data within your Business Central environment for the following purposes:

- **Inventory Classification**: Calculate ABC classifications based on revenue, profit, or cost data
- **Analysis and Reporting**: Generate reports showing inventory distribution and trends
- **Historical Tracking**: Maintain history of classification changes for audit purposes
- **Cycle Counting Integration**: Assign counting period codes based on ABC classifications
- **Performance Optimization**: Improve calculation efficiency and accuracy

### 3.2 Telemetry Data Usage

Telemetry data collected via Application Insights is used to:

- **Monitor app health**: Detect and diagnose technical issues
- **Improve performance**: Identify bottlenecks and optimize calculations
- **Enhance features**: Understand which features are most valuable to users
- **Fix bugs**: Investigate error reports and implement fixes
- **Plan updates**: Prioritize development based on usage patterns

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Business Central Data

All business data processed by the ABC Inventory Analysis is stored in your Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central database. This data is:

- **Stored in your tenant**: Remains within your Business Central environment
- **Controlled by you**: Subject to your Business Central security settings
- **Protected by Microsoft**: Benefits from Microsoft's enterprise-grade security measures
- **Backed up by Microsoft**: Included in Business Central's standard backup procedures

We do **not** maintain separate copies of your business data on external servers.

### 4.2 Telemetry Data

Telemetry data transmitted to Application Insights is:

- **Stored in Microsoft Azure**: European data centers (West Europe region)
- **Retained for 90 days**: Automatically deleted after retention period
- **Anonymized**: Contains no personally identifiable information
- **Encrypted in transit**: Uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Access-controlled**: Limited to authorized GMSOFT technical staff

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Business Central Data

Data stored in your Business Central environment (inventory transactions, ABC classifications, history records) is retained according to **your** data retention policies. We do not control or dictate retention periods for data within your Business Central tenant.

To delete ABC Inventory Analysis data from your Business Central environment:
- Uninstall the App from Business Central
- ABC-specific tables will be removed according to Business Central's app removal procedures

### 7.2 Telemetry Data

Telemetry data in Application Insights is automatically deleted after **90 days**. We do not maintain long-term archives of telemetry data.

## 9. International Data Transfers

### 9.1 Business Central Data

Your Business Central data is stored in the Microsoft Azure region you selected when setting up your Business Central environment. The ABC Inventory Analysis does not transfer this data outside of your Business Central tenant.

### 9.2 Telemetry Data

Telemetry data is transmitted to Microsoft Application Insights servers located in the **West Europe** region (Netherlands). Microsoft complies with EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Privacy Shield Frameworks and uses Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for international data transfers.

If you are located outside the European Economic Area, your telemetry data may be processed in the West Europe region, which may have different data protection laws than your country.
